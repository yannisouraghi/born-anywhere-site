Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Born Anywhere is published by Crocoding SAS, Rouen, France. This page explains what data the app uses, why, and where it goes.

In short
  • No account, no sign-up, no email address asked.
  • Your lives, journal and settings stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking across apps.
  • No location, camera, microphone or contacts.
  • The only outside service handles the in-app purchase (Legacy pack): RevenueCat, with Apple or Google for payment.

Data used

When you share a birth or your end-of-life card, your phone’s share sheet opens and you choose who receives it. The app sends nothing on its own.

Game statistics

Countries, cities and figures (births, mortality, schooling, income…) come from public sources such as the World Bank and the United Nations. They are built into the app; no personal data is used to compute them. Characters are fictional.

Legal bases and retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Purchase data is processed to perform the purchase contract and kept by RevenueCat, Apple or Google under their own obligations, including accounting.

Providers

  • RevenueCat, Inc.: in-app purchase management. RevenueCat privacy policy.
  • Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play): payment and app distribution.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, erase, object to or port the data about you by writing to contact@crocoding.com. Since game data never leaves your phone, you can also erase it yourself at any time. You may also contact the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

Children

The app has no account and does not knowingly collect data that identifies a child. Some stories deal with serious topics (war, illness, death); the “Soften sensitive topics” setting softens them.

Changes

This page may change with the app. The date at the top shows the latest version.
